# Gen Z in the workplace
### 1. What are some of the key characteristics of Gen Z as described in the article ?
In the article, Gen Z is described with strongly held values, which would result in them being more informed and accepting of certain groups, such as LGBTQ+, neurodivergent or disabled people.
Because of this, Gen Z would be more likely to work for companies that share their values.
Gen Z is also described as a generation that speaks its mind, is more likely to question established norms, and communicates more informally.
She is finally described as having good technology skills, and being heavily influenced by internet culture.

### 2. What challenges does Gen Z face in the current economic climate ?
Gen Z faces an unstable financial situation and an uncertain job market. They also have to deal with a reality that differs from their expectations and mental health struggles.

### 3. How does Stephanie Kelly suggest companies should adapt their management style to Gen Z preferences ?
Stephanie Kelly advocates more empathetic and inclusive management, including personalized support and open discussions, to better engage and retain employees.

### 4. What does Jason Fowler highlight as a key factor influencing Gen Z's job choices ?
Jason Fowler highlights that Gen Z places a strong emphasis on a company’s values, culture, and purpose when choosing a job. He cites research from EY indicating that 74% of Gen Z workers would quit their jobs if they found another role with a clearer sense of purpose.

### 5. TRUE OR FALSE: Gen Z is generally uninterested in learning new skills.
False ; Gen Z is highly interested in learning new skills, but they prefer self-directed, on-demand learning rather than traditional training methods.
>Gen Z also learns very differently from their older office counterparts. For instance, ‘CareerTok’ on TikTok has become instrumental in helping them with their workplace woes.


### 6. TRUE OR FALSE: Gen Z workers are comfortable navigating office politics and unspoken rules.
False ; Gen Z workers often challenge traditional workplace norms and unspoken rules.
>They question practices like lacking empathy for struggling colleagues, expecting employees to have no work-life balance while maintaining peak performance, and criticizing cultural symbols as being unprofessional. Gen Z challenges these norms.

### 2. What are the main concerns experts have about remote work for young employees?
Experts are concerned that young employees miss out on informal learning opportunities, struggle with communication norms, and may find it harder to build professional networks and leadership skills.

### 3. How can miscommunication be a problem in virtual work environments?
Miscommunication in virtual work environments can arise from difficulties in interpreting tone in emails, uncertainty about when to schedule meetings, and challenges in balancing visibility with productivity.
