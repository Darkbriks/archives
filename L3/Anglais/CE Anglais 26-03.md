## 9. A video résumé should replace a traditional written CV.
**False**
The document emphasizes that a video CV must be complementary to a written CV, particularly because recruiters spend very little time reading a traditional CV (the 11-second rule) and automated selection systems do not take videos into account.

## 10. Automated résumé selection systems can analyse video résumés.
**False**
The text specifies that these systems do not consider video CVs, thus risking making you *"disappear"* if you only send the video.

## 11. When filming, it is better to have a plain white wall behind you.
**False**
It is advisable to avoid a white wall or a background that is too neutral, as this is not very flattering for the image.

## 12. It is advised to speak quickly in a video CV to get all the information across.
**False**
The text recommends speaking more slowly and pausing carefully to facilitate editing and ensure good understanding.

## 13. It is acceptable for a video CV to be 3 minutes long.
**False**
The document advises limiting the length of the video CV to 1-2 minutes (except for specific positions)

## 14. An `mp4` file is the most common video format.
**True**
The document recommends the mp4 format, specifying that it is the most widely used format.

## 15. If you are nervous in front of a camera you should still create a video CV.
**False**
It is advised that if your nervousness affects your performance in front of the camera, it is better to opt for a traditional CV so as not to harm your image.

## 16. What is the "11-second rule" mentioned in the text ?
This is the average time that recruiters spend reading a traditional CV, which underlines the importance of quickly attracting their attention.

## 17. According to Samuel Valensi, what is the first question you should ask when planning a video CV ?
You need to ask yourself “Who are you addressing?”, that is, determine the profile of the targeted company or recruiter.

## 18. What is the advantage of using a tripod and lapel microphone when recording a video CV ?
They provide better image stability and superior sound quality, which improves the overall rendering of the video.

## 19. What should you include at the end of your video CV?
It is important to include your contact details and, possibly, a link to your portfolio or LinkedIn profile to make it easier for the recruiter to contact you.
